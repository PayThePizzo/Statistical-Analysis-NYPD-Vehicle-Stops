# Statistical-Analysis-NYPD-Vehicle-Stops

Predicting Excessive Enforcement Patterns in Police Behavior After Vehicles Stops in New York.

## About the project

The project is divided into 3 parts:

1. [Intro and Data Preparation](https://htmlpreview.github.io/?https://github.com/PayThePizzo/Statistical-Analysis-NYPD-Vehicle-Stops/blob/main/Intro-and-Data-Preparation.html) - We give an initial idea of what we want to do and how (mainly a discussion with very limited touch the data, only to add things and eventually correct obvious mistakes/change format/ identify or create target feature).
2. [EDA and Feature Engineering](https://htmlpreview.github.io/?https://github.com/PayThePizzo/Statistical-Analysis-NYPD-Vehicle-Stops/blob/main/EDA-and-Feature-Engineering.html) - We explore the features in 3 different ways. First we separately analyze them and try to grasp their meaning, their potential and possible outliers or other problematic aspects. Then we compare them with respect to the target feature, and try to understand if there is any notable relationship between the two of them. Also, we try to see whether there is evidence of correlation or collinearity between non-target features or other problematic behaviors they may introduce. Finally we explore the subsets of features that may be related (ex: geospatial features and command unit, or time of the day and some demographics), to find out whether some of them can be removed or replaced. Plus we try to investigate about multicollinearity and other complications.
3. [Model Selection and Evaluation](https://htmlpreview.github.io/?https://github.com/PayThePizzo/Statistical-Analysis-NYPD-Vehicle-Stops/blob/main/Model-Selection-and-Evaluation.html) - We try to select a subset of feature based on the previous analysis and an automatic selection (either through stepwise approaches or shrinkage methods). Then we perform an initial evaluation of the models' capabilities by a k-fold cross validation method to see how they perform and manage to explain the data. Finally we focus on a final evaluation with the chosen models on 2 different datasets and see whether were are in the position to give a definitive answer to our initial questions.
